*Uva* is Latin for grape.

I wanted a place with no rush: what I write, what I make and what I save, all ripening here slowly.

> New ones start out green and slowly turn purple.

That is why every entry has a small dot in front of it: green when it is fresh, turning purple over about six weeks. It does not mean good or bad — it is just a reminder that things need time.

There is no grand plan: I write down what comes to mind, share what I make, and bookmark what is worth keeping. Everything is written in Markdown, one file in Chinese and one in English. Push to GitHub and a new row appears here, and every entry gets a page of its own.
